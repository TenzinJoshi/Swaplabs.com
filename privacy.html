Information we collect

We collect account details, profile information, skill listings, messages, session records, reviews, and basic technical information required to operate the service.

How we use information

Information supports matching, communication, time-credit settlement, trust and safety, customer support, product improvement, and legal compliance.

When information is shared

Public profile details are visible to members. We use service providers for infrastructure and support. We do not sell personal information.

Messages, notifications, and video sessions

Direct messages stay inside the conversation shared by its participants. SwapBot may provide basic platform notices, but it does not copy or deliver the contents of one member’s message into the assistant conversation. A reported conversation can be reviewed by an authorized administrator for safety moderation.

Video rooms store invitations, waiting-room state, connection checks, and attendance confirmation. Camera, microphone, and screen media are exchanged by the participating browsers and are not recorded by SwapLabs.

Student and guardian safeguards

Accounts under 18 are private by default, hide precise location details, and allow direct messages or video sessions only after an accepted connection. Guardian details and consent status are stored for account review. Student Innovation submissions stay in specialist moderation until an authorized reviewer approves publication.

Your choices

You can edit profile visibility, communication preferences, location precision, analytics participation, and retention settings. The Profile page provides a complete JSON account export, downloadable CSV session history, immediate retention cleanup, correction controls, and permanent self-service account deletion.

Open privacy and data controls

Retention

Members can select standard, reduced, or minimal general retention and separate message and session-history periods. Some ledger, dispute, safety, and audit records may be retained or pseudonymized where required to preserve account safety and transaction integrity.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the Contact page.
