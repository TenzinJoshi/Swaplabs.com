Complaint details

Share enough detail to investigate without including unrelated sensitive information.

Authorized administrators can view the complete complaint, change its review status, and add private administrative notes.

Use Report for safety

Harassment, discrimination, fraud, impersonation, threats, unsafe behavior, and inappropriate content should use the confidential Report page.

Protect sensitive information

Do not include passwords, complete payment details, identity documents, health records, or information about another person that is not needed for the review.

Keep supporting evidence

Save relevant confirmations, dates, screenshots, and references. The team may ask for a specific item later through a secure route.

Prefer a question first?

If you mainly need guidance or clarification, Contact Us may resolve the issue faster without a formal complaint review.

Review process

A complaint moves through visible decision stages.

Administrators keep the submission, status, and internal review notes together so the record does not depend on memory.

Received

Reference issued

The complaint is saved with your account context where available, a timestamp, category, and priority.

Triage

Scope and urgency checked

The team confirms whether the complaint route is correct and whether time-sensitive action is required.

Review

Relevant records considered

Account, session, workshop, support, or policy context is checked with the minimum necessary access.

Outcome

Decision and next step

The response explains the action, reasoning, correction where appropriate, and any available review route.

Service standards

What you can reasonably expect from the process.

Targets can vary with complexity, but the team should communicate when a review needs more time.

Acknowledgement

A complaint reference is issued immediately after successful submission. Human acknowledgement is targeted within two business days.

Proportionate investigation

The team should review enough evidence to answer the complaint without collecting excessive personal data.

Reasoned response

An outcome should state what was reviewed, what was decided, and what correction or further action follows.

Review option

If you believe relevant evidence or policy was missed, explain the specific point that should be reconsidered.
