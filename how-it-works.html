A simple exchange model

From profile to useful session.

SwapLabs keeps the process clear, fair, and flexible for teachers and learners.

1

Share your skill map

Add skills you teach, goals you are working toward, availability, and preferred session format.

2

Review compatible people

Matches consider complementary needs, experience, schedule, teaching style, and location.

3

Agree on the exchange

Use secure messaging to define a useful goal, session length, and meeting method.

4

Meet, confirm, and review

After the session, both people confirm the time and leave private and public feedback.

Fair by design

One hour taught equals one hour earned.

Time credits separate teaching from direct barter. You can teach Python to one person and spend the credit learning pottery from someone else.
