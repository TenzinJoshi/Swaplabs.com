Choose the right route

Questions, complaints, and safety reports are different.

Sending the right kind of request helps the team respond with the right process.

  • Contact: questions, partnerships, press, access, and general help
  • Complaint: a formal concern where you want a reviewed resolution
  • Report: harassment, fraud, dangerous behavior, or unsafe content
  • Feedback: ideas, ratings, and optional public testimonials

Send your message

Fields marked required help us route and answer your request.

Your message and account reference, if signed in, are visible to authorized administrators for response and follow-up.

Response expectations

  • Account and general support: one business day
  • Partnership and program questions: two business days
  • Privacy requests: acknowledged within two business days
  • Urgent safety concerns: use the Report route

Include useful context

Share the affected page or feature, relevant dates, a session or workshop reference, and the outcome you are trying to reach. Avoid unnecessary sensitive data.

Accessibility support

Tell us the barrier, device or assistive technology involved, and the task you could not complete. We will focus on a practical route forward.

What happens next

Your message becomes a trackable support record.

The response process is designed to keep ownership clear without asking you to repeat the same context.

Step 1

Reference created

The platform saves your message and returns a unique confirmation reference.

Step 2

Request routed

An administrator can see the category, account context, message, and time submitted.

Step 3

Owner responds

The team records status and private follow-up notes while working toward an answer.

Step 4

Next action clarified

You receive an answer, a request for safe additional context, or a link to the correct formal route.
