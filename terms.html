Time credits are a platform accounting unit, not money, securities, or transferable cash value. Credits may be adjusted when sessions are disputed or reversed.

Members choose whom to meet and remain responsible for evaluating a session’s suitability. SwapLabs provides platform tools but does not guarantee outcomes.

Member content

You retain ownership of content you create and grant SwapLabs permission to display it as needed to operate the service.

Suspension and termination

Accounts may be limited or closed for policy violations, safety risks, fraud, or misuse. Members may close their own accounts at any time.
